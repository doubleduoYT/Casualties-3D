## Introduction

The Casualties Unknown Fan Project aims to create a **non-commercial, open-source fan work**, with development guided by shared goals and contributions. **Casualties: Unknown** is created by **Orsoniks**.

The team's description of the game: a grimdark survival game in which players guide **Experiments** into the depths to recover lost cargo, overcoming hostile creatures, traps, and environmental dangers.

## Art direction

The original game references pixel-art graphics and gritty yet sometimes vibrant palettes. Lighting, shaders, and environmental presentation reinforce isolation. The fan project's 3D direction is:

- PS1-era-inspired **low-poly models** with visible, sharp geometry.
- Pixel-art texturing where useful; restrained material texture complexity.
- Lower screen resolution / intentionally limited detail.
- Lighting and color choices emphasizing atmosphere.
- Clearer object silhouettes, with outlines or other legibility cues where suitable.

See [ART_DIRECTION.md](ART_DIRECTION.md).

## Music and sound effects

The master document references the following original game tracks as **listening/stylistic references only**:

- *Disturbing Discovery*
- *Radiation pulse*
- *Gaze Into the Pit*
- *Dying ..! Death*

This list **does not imply permission to reuse the recordings**.

The intended sound includes atmospheric lo-fi/synthwave-influenced music, bitcrushing, static, situational filtering, area-dependent score transitions, realistic environmental noises, and context-dependent hearing effects. In VR or high-volume audio, tester comfort and controllable dynamics are important.

See [SOUND_DESIGN.md](SOUND_DESIGN.md).

## AI-generated / AI-modified code and assets

The master document permits **vibe coding** (AI-assisted implementation) but notes it can introduce errors. Every use of an AI tool **for any reason** should be disclosed so future team members can review and maintain the work.

See [AI_CONTRIBUTIONS.md](AI_CONTRIBUTIONS.md).

## Ownership and publication goals

The master document says work produced by individual contributors remains their own. Reuse elsewhere requires **explicit permission from the relevant contributor**. The project plans a non-commercial open-source release.

However, the document alone does not establish a specific open-source license, permissions to redistribute original-game material, or any transfer of contributor rights. Those still require agreement and evidence. See [ASSET_POLICY.md](ASSET_POLICY.md).
